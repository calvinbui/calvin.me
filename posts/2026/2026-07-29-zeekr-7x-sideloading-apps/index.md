I found a way to sideload Android apps directly onto the infotainment system in my [Zeekr 7X](https://www.zeekrlife.com/en-au/models/zeekr-7x/), even though the car does not include the Google Play Store.

<!-- more -->

I previously covered the financial side of electric vehicles with my [Novated Lease Reviewer Skill](/novated-lease-reviewer-skill/). This time, I am dealing with the software inside one instead.

The process involves escaping the restricted App Labs browser sandbox before installing a normal browser and downloading APK files. It is long and fiddly enough that a video is much easier to follow than a written guide, so I recorded the complete process below.

This is an unofficial method and is not supported by Zeekr. Follow it at your own risk.
